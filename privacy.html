Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · What is kept where, in plain words.

No accountWe never ask for your name, email or phone number.
Your own iCloudYour save's backup lives in your account; we can't see it.
Anonymous statsTo improve the game; you can turn them off.
No ads, no trackingNo purchases, and your data is never sold.

1. Your save (on your phone)

Your progress (heroes, items, buildings, settings and the captain and town names you enter) is stored on your phone. It is not sent to us.

2. iCloud backup (in your own iCloud)

If iCloud is turned on on your iPhone, the game keeps a copy of your save through Apple's iCloud service, in your own iCloud account. This lets you continue after reinstalling the game or moving to a new phone with the same Apple ID.

  • The backup is handled by Apple and stored with your Apple ID. We cannot see or access it.
  • It contains only the game save described above.
  • If you are not signed in to iCloud, no backup is made and the game works normally.

3. Anonymous usage data

We use Unity Gaming Services (Unity Analytics) to understand which stages are difficult and where players get stuck. Collected data:

  • in-game events (e.g. clearing a stage, opening a pack) and your current stage,
  • device model, OS version, game version, language and approximate country,
  • a random, anonymous player ID created by the game.

This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising and is never sold. The game also downloads settings such as event dates and in-game letters; this sends no personal data. How Unity processes data: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. Your choices

  • Settings → Anonymous usage data: turn off to stop data collection.
  • Settings → Reset progress: deletes your save on the phone and its iCloud backup.
  • Settings → Delete account and data: deletes your save, your iCloud backup, the anonymous player ID on the server and requests deletion of your usage data.
  • Uninstalling the game removes the game data from your phone. Your iCloud backup stays in your iCloud until you delete it in the game or turn off iCloud for the app in iPhone Settings.

5. Children, changes and contact

The game is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect personal information. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: bavulkasabasi@gmail.com
