Privacy Policy

Bavul Kasabası · Last updated: October 24, 2026

Bavul Kasabası is a free mobile game developed by Umut Durna. This page explains what data the game processes and your choices.

In short

  • You don't need an account, name, email or phone number to play.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking for advertising.
  • Anonymous usage data is collected to improve the game; you can turn it off in Settings.

Stored on your device

Your progress (heroes, items, buildings, settings and the captain and town names you enter) is stored only on your phone and is not sent to us.

Anonymous usage data

We use Unity Gaming Services (Unity Analytics) to understand which stages are difficult and where players get stuck. Collected data:

  • in-game events (e.g. clearing a stage, opening a pack) and your current stage,
  • device model, OS version, game version, language and approximate country,
  • a random, anonymous player ID created by the game.

This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising and is never sold. How Unity processes data: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Your choices

  • Turn off Settings → Anonymous usage data to stop data collection.
  • Settings → Delete account and data deletes your local save, the anonymous player ID on the server and requests deletion of your usage data.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all game data from your phone.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
