About

A suitcase, an inventor and lands waiting to be rescued.

The story

In faraway lands lived a wandering inventor named Odo. Odo shrank lands in danger and kept them safe inside his suitcases. Until one night, the Shadow Emperor came.

The suitcases were scattered far and wide, and the lands inside fell into darkness. Odo was left with just one: a worn old leather suitcase. And a young captain to travel with him… You.

In Suitcase Town you build your own town inside that old suitcase, collect heroes and rescue the suitcases one by one from the Shadow Emperor.

From the developer

I'm making Suitcase Town to be something you can enjoy on the subway or during a coffee break, yet a journey that lasts for months. The game is in early access: I add something new every week and players' feedback sets the course. No ads, no purchases; just the game.

Umut Recep Durna · independent developer
