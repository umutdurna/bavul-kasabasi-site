Updates

Suitcase Town is in early access and grows regularly. Here is what's new.

Grand Opening: Seasons 3, 4 and 5 OUT NOW Version 1.1 · November 2026

Odo's journey just got three seasons longer! The Dream Suitcases, the Deep Suitcases and the Star Suitcases are waiting for you.

NEW ADVENTURE

  • 18 new suitcases, 180 new stages and 18 new bosses.
  • Every season has its own story, new chapter scenes and its own rules.
  • Mythic heroes unique to each season.

GEAR, REBUILT

  • Gear now grows with your heroes: at every level about a third of your power comes from gear.
  • Two new slots: Ring and Charm, wearable by every class.
  • From 18 items to 66: war hammer, spear, longbow, mace, spirit lantern, Odo's Pocket Watch, Suitcase Key and more. New suitcases unlock new items.
  • Some items have their own perk: stun, life steal, armor pierce, acting first…
  • An item's rarity now shows in your hero's hand too.

HERO POWER

  • Mythic Seal: when the Mythic pack gives no Mythic you earn a seal; pick any Mythic for 60 seals.
  • Hero Essence and Awakening II and III: copies past 5 stars turn into essence and make signature skills even stronger.

EVENTS

  • Winter Festival (December 22 – January 5): a snowy town, a 14-day reward calendar and the Snow Guardian event.
  • Season League: a Game Center leaderboard that resets every 4 weeks, with everyone starting on the same day.

SMOOTHER PLAY

  • Battles flow better at normal speed.
  • Lists no longer jump to the top when you level up heroes, in the inventory or when trading.
  • Rarity filter and one-tap "pick the weakest 4" when trading.
  • Collecting 6 items at once from the Workshop now fits on screen.
  • The Enchant button is right where you need it.
  • Town buildings now face you, and heroes no longer walk through them.

Season 2: The Dark Suitcases Version 1.0 · November 2026

The Shadow Emperor has fallen, but he was only a puppet. The sky has torn open, and Odo's lost suitcases have appeared, swallowed by darkness. The manor is now a ship. Let's set sail!

A NEW ADVENTURE

  • 6 new suitcases, 60 new stages: Sky Islands, Cog City, Golden Desert, Mirror Caves, Sunken City and the Dark Workshop.
  • Every suitcase has its own rule: Wind shuffles the turn order, Tide swaps the front and back rows, Sandstorm hides enemy health, and in the Mirror your enemies copy your team…
  • 6 new bosses, each with a unique ability. A two-phase battle awaits you in the finale.
  • The story goes on: the Season 1 finale, new chapter scenes and a big secret.

NEW HEROES AND POWER

  • Mythic heroes: Sky Pirate, Cog Knight, Sand Sage and Coral Barbarian. Each has its own stat profile and joins close to your team's level.
  • Awakening: when a hero reaches 5 stars, its signature skill awakens and grows 25% stronger.
  • The Harbor: the manor ship's home port comes to your town. Each level gives your whole team attack and health.

ENCHANTMENTS

  • Enchant fusion: upgrade 3 enchantments of a kind to the next quality, or fuse two types into a twin enchantment.
  • Team chemistry in 8 slots: weapon and off-hand enchantments both count.

ODO'S MAP

  • Travel between seasons and return to the suitcases you rescued; replay old bosses and rewatch the story.
  • Weekly bosses: beat the bosses of rescued suitcases every week to earn stamps.

SMOOTHER PLAY

  • More back-and-forth battles: a single hit takes far less health, so both sides last longer.
  • Batch pack opening: open 3 packs at once or see them all on one screen.
  • Game Center leaderboards: captain level, team power, stage and the weekly Shadow Tower.
  • Captain's Log: achievements, leaderboards and settings in one place.
  • A capped hero's XP goes to their teammates.

GIFTS

  • Captains who update get 2,000 gold, 15 stamps and an Advanced Hero Pack in their mailbox.
  • Captains who finished Season 1 also get a veteran's chest: a Legendary Hero Pack and 10 stamps.
  • Captains who travelled the Endless Road move to the start of Season 2, and the road they covered is paid back in stamps.

Enchantments, Team Chemistry and the Suitcase Theme Version 0.9 · October 2026

  • Enchantments: put a Sun, Night, Storm or Nature enchantment on your gear. Higher quality, stronger item.
  • Team chemistry: a team sharing an enchantment type grows stronger together: shields, critical damage, health or healing.
  • The Enchanter: a new building in your town. Enchant gear and remove enchantments any time; nothing is lost.
  • New packs: the Daily Pack, Enchantment Pack, Enchantment Pouch and the weekly Chemistry Pack.
  • Special cards: sometimes a Shiny, Gold Leaf or Legendary Suitcase card comes out of a pack.
  • Achievements: rewarded goals for battles, the tower, your collection and enchanting.
  • Suitcase theme: leather, cream paper and brass; every screen in the game was redesigned.

Shadow Tower and Mailbox Version 0.8 · October 2026

  • Shadow Tower: a 50-floor tower that rises again every week. Enemies are the same for everyone, and your best record is never erased.
  • Mailbox: letters and gifts from us. The first one comes with a welcome gift!
  • Signature skills: every rare hero now has a special skill of their own.
  • Hidden abilities: some enemies can surprise you in the middle of a battle. Stay sharp!
  • Your save is safe: your progress is backed up to your own iCloud.
  • Rarity guide: see at a glance which cards are worth more.
  • The Early Access Week event was rebalanced with daily tickets for a longer adventure.

Odo's Story Version 0.7 · October 2026

  • Opening story: the tale of Odo and his lost suitcases, told in illustrated scenes.
  • Chapter scenes: each new suitcase opens with the story of its region.
  • Bosses speak up: every boss enters with a challenge and a trick of its own.
  • Odo's Map: see where you came from and where you're headed.
  • Early Access Week: the first event, with a hero you can only get there.
  • Elite enemies and building bonuses that permanently power up your team.

Six Suitcases Version 0.6 · October 2026

  • Three new regions: Emerald Forest, Ash Volcano and Shadow Palace. Six suitcases and 60 stages in total.
  • An army for every region: bandits, frost knights, forest shades and more.
  • Nine new heroes, and you can now rename your heroes.
  • Language choice: Turkish or English, regardless of your phone's language.
  • More tactical battles: power really matters now.

Early Access Begins Version 0.5 · October 2026

  • The first version of Suitcase Town: town, heroes, packs and turn-based tactical battles.
  • Academy, Research Workshop, daily quests and expeditions.
  • Turkish and English.
