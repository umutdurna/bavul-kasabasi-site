Updates

Bavul Kasabası is in early access and grows regularly. Here is what's new.

Shadow Tower and Mailbox NEW Version 0.8 · October 2026

  • Shadow Tower: a 50-floor tower that rises again every week. Enemies are the same for everyone, and your best record is never erased.
  • Mailbox: letters and gifts from us. The first one comes with a welcome gift!
  • Signature skills: every rare hero now has a special skill of their own.
  • Hidden abilities: some enemies can surprise you in the middle of a battle. Stay sharp!
  • Your save is safe: your progress is backed up to your own iCloud.
  • Rarity guide: see at a glance which cards are worth more.
  • The Early Access Week event was rebalanced with daily tickets for a longer adventure.

Odo's Story Version 0.7 · October 2026

  • Opening story: the tale of Odo and his lost suitcases, told in illustrated scenes.
  • Chapter scenes: each new suitcase opens with the story of its region.
  • Bosses speak up: every boss enters with a challenge and a trick of its own.
  • Odo's Map: see where you came from and where you're headed.
  • Early Access Week: the first event, with a hero you can only get there.
  • Elite enemies and building bonuses that permanently power up your team.

Six Suitcases Version 0.6 · October 2026

  • Three new regions: Emerald Forest, Ash Volcano and Shadow Palace. Six suitcases and 60 stages in total.
  • An army for every region: bandits, frost knights, forest shades and more.
  • Nine new heroes, and you can now rename your heroes.
  • Language choice: Turkish or English, regardless of your phone's language.
  • More tactical battles: power really matters now.

Early Access Begins Version 0.5 · October 2026

  • The first version of Bavul Kasabası: town, heroes, packs and turn-based tactical battles.
  • Academy, Research Workshop, daily quests and expeditions.
  • Turkish and English.
