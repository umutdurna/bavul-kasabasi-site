<br>

A wandering inventor named **Odo** shrank lands in danger and kept them safe in his suitcases, until the **Shadow Emperor** scattered them far and wide. Now he has just one old leather suitcase… and you.

**Suitcase Town** is a free fantasy strategy game for iPhone:

- 🏘️ **Build your town:** every building unlocks a feature and a permanent team bonus.
- 🛡️ **Collect heroes:** six rarities from Common to Mythic, signature skills that awaken at 5 stars.
- ⚔️ **Fight with tactics:** formation, class advantage and bosses with their own tricks.
- 🧳 **Rescue 12 suitcases:** Season 1 *The Shadow Emperor* (stages 1–60) and Season 2 *The Dark Suitcases* (stages 61–120, version 1.0).
- 🏰 **Shadow Tower, events and Game Center leaderboards.**

No ads. Turkish and English.
